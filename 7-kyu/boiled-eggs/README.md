<h2><a href=https://www.codewars.com/kata/52b5247074ea613a09000164/train/rust target="_blank">Boiled Eggs</a></h2><h3>7 kyu</h3><p>You are the greatest chef on earth. No one boils eggs like you! Your restaurant is always full of guests, who love your boiled eggs. But when there is a greater order of boiled eggs, you need some time, because you have only one pot for your job. How much time do you need?</p><h2 id="your-task">Your Task</h2><p>Implement a function, which takes a non-negative integer, representing the number of eggs to boil. It must return the time in minutes (integer), which it takes to have all the eggs boiled.</p><h2 id="rules">Rules</h2><ul><li>you can put at most 8 eggs into the pot at once</li><li>it takes 5 minutes to boil an egg</li><li>we assume, that the water is boiling all the time (no time to heat up)</li><li>for simplicity we also don't consider the time it takes to put eggs into the pot or get them out of it</li></ul><h2 id="example-input----output">Example (Input --&gt; Output)</h2><pre><code>0 --&gt; 05 --&gt; 510 --&gt; 10</code></pre>
